## Feedback System
- **Model**: `Feedback` and `FeedbackAnalysis` schema models with `organizationId` multi-tenant keys.
- **Inbox**: Scoped stream displaying customer, message, source, sentiment badge, score, status, date, and themes with search & filters.
- **Detail Page**: Contact metadata, raw message, and AI Intelligence summary with status action buttons.
- **Add Feedback**: Form creating feedback under the authenticated user's organization with optional auto AI analysis.
- **Search & Filters**: Real-time search across name, email, message, category, theme; filters by sentiment, status, source.
- **CSV Import**: Modal & endpoint parsing CSV uploads and bulk inserting valid rows under the organization.

## AI Analysis
- **Sentiment**: Classified as `POSITIVE`, `NEUTRAL`, or `NEGATIVE`.
- **Score**: Quantitative rating from 0 (strongly negative) to 100 (strongly positive).
- **Confidence**: Statistical confidence score (e.g., 94%).
- **Category**: Classification into Performance, Support, Pricing, UX, Bugs, Feature Requests, or General.
- **Themes**: Automatically extracted theme tags (e.g., "Performance & Load Times").
- **Summary**: Concise executive summary generated by AI.

## Emerging Trends
Foundation implemented at `/dashboard/trends`. Aggregates feedback volume by theme, compares recent vs previous period volume (+15%, +22%), and tracks average theme sentiment scores.

## Multi-Tenancy
All feedback queries (`/api/feedback`, `/api/feedback/[id]`, `/api/feedback/import`, `/api/feedback/analyze-bulk`) extract `organizationId` directly from the authenticated session (`requireAuth()`). Frontend requests cannot specify or override `organizationId`, preventing cross-tenant data access.

## RBAC
Permissions enforced server-side:
- **ADMIN** & **MANAGER**: Full feedback creation, status updates, archiving, CSV import, AI analysis, bulk AI execution.
- **ANALYST**: View feedback inbox, run AI analysis, view analytics & trends.
- **SUPPORT**: View permitted feedback inbox, update status (Reviewed/Resolved).
